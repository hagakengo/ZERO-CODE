# TEST A — Back Close-Up Prompt

> Version: 0.1  
> Purpose: D1 continuity test  
> Asset status: D1 v1.1 Production Master Candidate  
> Test type: simple controlled shot

---

## Objective

Verify whether the video model can preserve the D1 emblem during a simple, low-motion shot.

This test does not judge beauty first.

It judges continuity first.

---

## Prompt

Create a cinematic close-up video of the exact same protagonist and backpack shown in the reference image.

The protagonist remains completely still and faces away from the camera.

The camera performs only a very slow push-in toward the backpack.

The ZERO CODE D1 emblem is a fixed physical emblem already attached to the backpack.

Preserve its exact silhouette, proportions, asymmetry, broken outer zero, rising diagonal A-like axis, central cyan core, lower-right fracture, orientation, size, and placement.

Do not redesign, reinterpret, complete, simplify, symmetrize, rotate, reconstruct, or transform the emblem.

The cyan light remains subtle and steady.

Dark futuristic city at night, wet atmosphere, subtle fog, shallow depth of field.

No character movement.
No turning.
No camera rotation.
No camera shake.
No cuts.
No additional characters.
No new accessories.

DO NOT REDESIGN THE D1 EMBLEM.
FILM IT EXACTLY AS IT EXISTS IN THE REFERENCE IMAGE.

---

## Negative instructions

Avoid:

- emblem transformation
- animated logo formation
- new emblem design
- completing the circle
- symmetrical logo cleanup
- extra neon lines on protagonist clothing
- character turn
- walking
- camera orbit
- fast zoom
- handheld shake
- text overlay
- additional symbols
- random UI panels

---

## Evaluation checklist

After generating, evaluate:

1. BROKEN ZERO
2. A AXIS
3. LOWER-RIGHT FRACTURE
4. CENTRAL CORE
5. BACKPACK PLACEMENT
