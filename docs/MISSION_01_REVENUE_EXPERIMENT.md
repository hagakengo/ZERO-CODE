# MISSION 01 — First verified ¥1

## Outcome
Generate **at least ¥1 of actual, attributable revenue** from ZERO CODE activity. Views, clicks, estimated affiliate commissions, and test transactions do not count as earned revenue.

## Hypotheses (not facts)
1. A useful free AI workflow or tutorial can attract qualified visitors.
2. A clearly disclosed, relevant affiliate offer or a small original digital product may convert some visitors.
3. A simple, single call-to-action per content item may be easier to evaluate than multiple competing offers.

## Smallest safe experiment
1. Choose **one** audience pain point and **one** monetization offer. Check the program's terms and applicable disclosure requirements before publishing.
2. Publish **one** genuinely useful tutorial or demonstration with a transparent CTA; avoid income guarantees and fabricated results.
3. Use a tagged link where the platform permits it. Do not collect unnecessary personal information.
4. Log the publication date, channel, destination, and the offer's actual costs.
5. Review measured impressions, clicks, confirmed conversions, confirmed revenue, and expenses after an agreed observation window.
6. Decide to iterate, stop, or expand based on evidence, not vanity metrics.

**Rules:** Unknown metrics stay unknown, not zero. Do not record pending affiliate commissions as paid revenue. Record refunds/chargebacks. Net result = confirmed revenue minus attributable costs. No screenshots, credentials, or customer PII in this repository.

## Decision gate
- Before publication: human reviews offer, claims, affiliate disclosures, and destination.
- Before spending: explicit human approval for paid tools, ads, or subscriptions.
- Before claiming MISSION 01 complete: independently verify the transaction or confirmed affiliate payout according to the chosen definition.
- Production credentials, account connections, and paid promotions are never automated without approval.

## Engineering priority
Keep CI and deployment safe, but avoid building additional analytics dashboards before there is a real distribution channel, a monetization offer, and at least one measurable experiment.
