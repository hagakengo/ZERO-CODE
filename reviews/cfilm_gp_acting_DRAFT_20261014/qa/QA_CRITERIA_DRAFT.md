# Independent QA criteria — DRAFT/HOLD

2026-10-14 JST. Read repository AGENTS, philosophy, production rules, context, README, production log and roadmap plus prior model-cleanup QA. This lane does not author the animation.

Reference: C/FILM top portrait from A_B_C_comparison_DRAFT.png. The C candidate is not approved MASTER. Current user explicitly authorized visible-face C production tests; old anonymous silhouette rule is not being amended for canon.

1. Identity: compare face boundary, narrow jaw, tilted half eyes, inter-eye distance, asymmetric central fringe, side tufts, nose and mouth against the exact C crop. A prettier new face is not identity success.
2. Fixed head: silhouette, jaw and hair pixels should stay stable. Moving pupils/eyelids must not drag face contours or change head angle.
3. Eyelids: dedicated closed curves, aligned to the original eye slope. No vertical compression of an eye image. Inspect lower-lid residue, original iris/eye line, skin repair boundary, fringe occlusion and clipping.
4. Acting: read gaze → blink → small notice in actual sequence; timing and smoothness cannot be established by frame count alone. Hair continuity is separate from hair follow-through; fixed hair passes continuity only.
5. Technical: normal 24 frames / 24 fps / 1 second; half speed 48 frames / 24 fps / 2 seconds; full decode; editable native Grease Pencil layers and saved project reopen. Video raster background alone is not GP character reconstruction.
6. Provenance: record human hand drawings, agent-authored contour designs, generated frame drawings and numeric interpolation separately. Repeated frames are exposure holds, not new drawings.

User normal-speed viewing and approval are pending. Static-frame inspection and full decode are not real-time continuous viewing. Any unachieved identity or acting quality remains HOLD even if technical validation passes.
