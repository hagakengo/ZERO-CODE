# ZERO CODE Teaser 00 — CapCut Edit Guide

> Title: ¥0から、起動する。  
> Status: EDIT READY  
> Target length: 15–20 sec  
> Format: 9:16 vertical

---

## 1. Purpose

This is not EP.01.

This is the first public-facing teaser that announces the world of ZERO CODE.

Goal:

- show the hooded protagonist from behind
- reveal the D1 emblem on the backpack
- imply system activation
- show the real starting status: followers 0, revenue ¥0
- end with Mission 01

---

## 4. Text rules

Use text in post-production, not generated video.

Priority:

1. readable
2. minimal
3. system-like
4. not decorative

Avoid:

- too much Japanese text at once
- busy subtitles
- flashy TikTok-style stickers
- unrelated sound effects

---

## 5. Recommended CapCut settings

Project:

```text
Aspect ratio: 9:16
Resolution: 1080x1920 if available
Frame rate: 24 or 30 fps
Length: 15–20 seconds
```

Color:

- dark blue / black base
- cyan highlights
- do not over-saturate

Effects:

- subtle glitch
- scan line
- light pulse
- fade to black

Avoid:

- big shake
- rapid zoom
- meme effects
- bright rainbow effects

---

## 6. Quality gate before export

Before exporting, confirm:

- protagonist does not turn around
- face is not visible
- D1 stays on backpack
- D1 does not become a random logo
- no extra character appears
- text is readable on phone
- total runtime is under 20 sec unless intentionally extended
- video feels like a teaser, not a full explanation
