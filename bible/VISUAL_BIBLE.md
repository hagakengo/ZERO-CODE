# ZERO CODE — VISUAL BIBLE

> Version: 0.1  
> Status: DRAFT  
> This document defines the visual truth of the project.

---

## 1. Protagonist

### Silhouette

- adult male-presenting
- slim-to-average build
- anonymous
- face hidden
- oversized hoodie silhouette
- cargo pants
- angular tech backpack

### Materials

- hoodie: matte black
- pants: charcoal black
- backpack: structured technical charcoal
- hardware: gunmetal
- shoes: black

### Lighting

> THE PROTAGONIST DOES NOT GLOW. ONLY ZERO CODE GLOWS.

No neon clothing trim.

In very dark scenes, the body may almost disappear while the emblem remains visible.

---

## 2. ZERO CODE emblem

Current asset:

**D1 v1.1 — Production Master Candidate**

Visual anchors:

- broken asymmetric zero
- rising A-like diagonal axis
- cyan core
- lower-right fracture
- interrupted orbit/path

Visual intention:

Not a clean logo.

It should feel like an unknown, damaged, reconstructed code trace.

> 欠けていることが、完成形である。

Do not “fix” its brokenness.

---

## 3. Backpack

- black/charcoal tech backpack
- angular geometry
- compact futuristic form
- no weapons
- no decorative clutter

Before activation:

- no emblem

After activation:

- D1 fixed at canonical placement
- steady cyan glow
- no random movement or redesign

## 4. World

### AI CITY

Mood:

- monumental
- mysterious
- system-controlled
- overwhelming scale

Materials / atmosphere:

- dark architecture
- wet surfaces
- cyan light
- electric blue
- sparse orange accents
- fog
- god rays
- reflective streets

The protagonist should feel small relative to the city.

## 5. ¥0 monument

¥0 is a critical recurring visual for EP.01.

Rules:

- always unmistakably readable as ¥0 before destruction
- maintain silhouette during crack buildup
- cracks should belong to the object
- avoid generic lightning replacing the shape
- shattering should happen only after identity is established

---

## 6. HUD / UI

Style:

- translucent
- cyan/blue
- minimal
- readable
- game/system interface
- not cluttered

Likely recurring labels:

- SYSTEM ONLINE
- PLAYER STATUS
- LV.
- FOLLOWERS
- TOTAL REVENUE
- MISSION
- SELECT YOUR ROUTE

Critical exact text should be composed in post-production.

---

## 7. Camera language

Preferred:

- slow push-in
- deliberate reveal
- large scale
- strong depth
- controlled motion

Avoid when continuity testing:

- fast spins
- aggressive handheld shake
- rapid cuts
- unnecessary character turns
- complex simultaneous motion

---

## 8. Color hierarchy

Primary:

- black
- dark blue

Energy / system:

- cyan
- electric blue

Accent:

- orange sparks/fire

Rule:

Cyan belongs primarily to ZERO CODE/system energy, not the protagonist's clothing.

---

## 9. Quality standard

Do not chase a beautiful single frame at the cost of continuity.

> 最高の1枚ではなく、最高の世界を壊さず作り続ける。
